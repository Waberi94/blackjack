A QUICK REFRESHER

Know your hand.

Get closer to 21 than the dealer, without going over.

The cards
Number cards count as shown. J, Q, and K are worth 10. An ace counts as 1 or 11, whichever helps your hand.
Your move
Hit takes another card. Stand keeps your total. Double doubles your bet, gives you exactly one more card, then stands. Double is available on your first two cards if you have enough chips.
The dealer
The dealer reveals their hidden card and draws until reaching 17 or more, including a soft 17.
The payout
A win pays 1:1. An ace and a 10-value card on the initial deal is blackjack and pays 3:2. A tie returns your bet. Dealer blackjack is checked on the initial deal.
This table
One freshly shuffled deck each round. Bets from 5 to 500 chips. No splitting, insurance, or surrender. Your practice balance resets when you reload.
